# MosaicAlert — Investor & Partner Brief

## Executive Summary
MosaicAlert is an AI-powered, offline-first mobile application designed to empower smallholder cassava farmers in Sub-Saharan Africa. Our tool provides real-time, on-device diagnosis of Cassava Mosaic Disease (CMD), enabling early intervention and protecting livelihoods.

## Problem
CMD is a devastating viral disease that can lead to 100% crop loss. Smallholder farmers often lack access to diagnostic expertise, and existing tools are either expensive or require reliable internet, which is unavailable in many rural areas.

## Solution
- **On-Device ML**: High-accuracy disease detection without internet.
- **Swahili-First UX**: Designed for farmers with localized voice guidance and actionable advisory.
- **Lightweight Design**: Optimized for the low-end Android devices common in rural markets.

## Impact
- **Food Security**: Protecting cassava, a primary food source for over 500 million people.
- **Economic Empowerment**: Reducing crop loss by up to 40% through early detection.
- **Data Insights**: Aggregated (privacy-preserving) heatmaps for NGOs and governments to track disease outbreaks.

## Business & Sustainability
- **B2G/B2NGO Model**: Licensing the analytics dashboard to government agricultural departments and NGOs.
- **Freemium App**: Core diagnosis is free for farmers; premium advisory and SMS alerts for a small subscription fee.
- **Ethical Data**: Building the largest, most diverse field dataset of cassava diseases in Africa.
