# MosaicAlert Pilot Recruitment Guide

## Objective
To recruit 50–200 smallholder cassava farmers to test the MosaicAlert MVP and provide real-world data to improve the model.

## Participant Criteria
- Owns or manages a cassava farm.
- Uses an Android smartphone (API 21+).
- Willing to provide feedback and share leaf images for research.

## Recruitment Steps
1. **Partner with Local NGOs**: Engage with agricultural extension services and local farmers' cooperatives.
2. **On-Boarding Workshop**: Conduct 30-minute training on how to use the app, emphasizing the "Scan Leaf" and "Voice Guidance" features.
3. **Consent Process**: Explain that images are uploaded only with their permission and will be used to improve the tool for all farmers.
4. **Data Collection**: Ask participants to scan at least 10 leaves per week (both healthy and symptomatic).

## Incentives
- Early access to the advisory tool.
- Free consultation with participating extension officers.
- Small mobile data stipends for those who opt-in to image uploads.

## Feedback Mechanism
- Weekly check-ins via SMS or local coordinators.
- In-app "Report Issue" button.
